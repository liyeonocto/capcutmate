Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

CapCut Mate ("CapCut Mate", "we", "our", or "the application") is an AI-assisted video editing application designed to help users organize media, analyze narration, match video footage, generate editing plans, and create rough-cut video projects.

This Privacy Policy explains what information CapCut Mate may access, how that information is used, how it is protected, and what choices users have regarding their data.

1. Information We Access

CapCut Mate only accesses information that is necessary to provide features requested by the user.

This may include:

  • Google account information required for authentication, such as account identity and email address.
  • Files stored in Google Drive when the user authorizes Google Drive access.
  • Video files, audio files, music files, images, narration recordings, subtitles, scripts, and other media selected or stored by the user.
  • File metadata such as file names, file IDs, file types, file sizes, folder locations, timestamps, and media duration.
  • Project settings and editing preferences such as aspect ratio, editing pace, subtitle preferences, music preferences, and voice-cleanup settings.
  • AI-generated metadata such as scene descriptions, visual tags, transcript segments, keywords, emotional tone, suggested footage, subtitle timing, and rough-cut editing instructions.

2. Google Drive Access

If a user connects Google Drive, CapCut Mate may request permission to view, create, edit, organize, download, or upload files in the user's Google Drive.

Google Drive access is used only to provide CapCut Mate functionality, including:

  • Locating video and music assets selected for editing.
  • Reading media files needed for AI analysis or video processing.
  • Creating and maintaining media-library indexes.
  • Saving project-related information.
  • Saving generated rough cuts or exported files.
  • Updating or organizing files when explicitly required by the user.

CapCut Mate does not access Google Drive for advertising, marketing, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

3. How Google User Data Is Used

Information received from Google APIs is used only to provide or improve user-facing CapCut Mate features requested by the user.

CapCut Mate's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is not sold.

Google user data is not used for personalized advertising.

Google user data is not transferred to advertising platforms or data brokers.

Google user data is not used to determine creditworthiness, lending eligibility, insurance eligibility, or other unrelated user profiling.

4. AI Processing

CapCut Mate may use artificial intelligence services to perform tasks such as:

  • Speech-to-text transcription.
  • Understanding narration and scripts.
  • Analyzing video scenes and visual content.
  • Generating semantic tags and media descriptions.
  • Matching narration segments with appropriate footage.
  • Selecting or recommending background music.
  • Generating subtitles and emphasized text.
  • Generating editing plans and rough-cut timelines.

When necessary to perform these functions, relevant portions of media, text, images, transcripts, or metadata may be securely transmitted to third-party AI service providers, including OpenAI or other service providers used by CapCut Mate.

Only data reasonably necessary for the requested processing should be sent to these services.

5. Media Processing

Video and audio files may need to be temporarily downloaded or copied to CapCut Mate's processing environment in order to perform operations such as:

  • Audio cleanup.
  • Speech transcription.
  • Frame extraction.
  • Scene analysis.
  • Video rendering.
  • Subtitle generation.
  • Audio mixing.
  • Rough-cut generation.

Temporary processing copies are intended only for performing the requested editing operation and should not be retained longer than reasonably necessary for processing, troubleshooting, security, or project recovery.

6. Media Library Indexing

CapCut Mate may analyze media files and create reusable metadata so that the same file does not need to be fully analyzed every time it is used.

This metadata may include:

  • Google Drive file ID.
  • File name and file type.
  • Media duration.
  • Scene description.
  • Objects or subjects appearing in the media.
  • Mood and visual style.
  • Camera motion or scene movement.
  • Semantic keywords.
  • Suggested editing use cases.

This indexing information is used only to make future searches and editing operations faster and more accurate.

7. Data Storage

Depending on the feature being used, CapCut Mate may store limited project information in databases or secure application storage.

This may include:

  • Project records.
  • Media file IDs and metadata.
  • Transcripts.
  • Editing timelines.
  • AI-generated media tags.
  • Processing status.
  • User preferences.

Original media may remain stored in the user's Google Drive rather than being permanently copied into CapCut Mate's own storage whenever the application architecture allows.

8. OAuth Tokens and Authentication

CapCut Mate may use Google OAuth authorization to connect to Google Drive.

Access tokens and refresh tokens may be stored securely when required so the application can maintain an authorized Google Drive connection.

OAuth credentials are used only to provide authorized application functions.

Passwords for Google accounts are never collected or stored by CapCut Mate.

9. Data Sharing

CapCut Mate does not sell personal information or user files.

Data may be shared with service providers only when required to provide the requested application functionality.

Examples may include:

  • Cloud hosting providers.
  • Database providers.
  • AI processing providers.
  • Media-processing infrastructure.
  • Google APIs used to access Google Drive.

These providers receive only information reasonably necessary to perform the relevant service.

10. Advertising

CapCut Mate does not use Google Drive data, private user media, transcripts, or project information for advertising.

CapCut Mate does not sell this information to advertisers or use it to create advertising profiles.

11. AI Model Training

CapCut Mate does not intentionally use private Google Drive files or private user media to train a general-purpose CapCut Mate AI model.

Third-party AI providers may process submitted content according to their own applicable service agreements, privacy policies, and API data-handling terms.

12. Data Retention

CapCut Mate retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, maintain project functionality, comply with legal obligations, protect application security, or resolve technical problems.

Temporary media-processing files may be deleted after processing is complete.

Reusable media metadata may be retained longer so that previously analyzed assets do not need to be analyzed again.

13. User Control and Data Deletion

Users may stop CapCut Mate from accessing Google Drive by revoking the application's Google account authorization.

Google account permissions can generally be reviewed through the user's Google Account security and third-party access settings.

Users may also request deletion of CapCut Mate project data, transcripts, stored media metadata, or other information associated with their use of the application.

14. Security

CapCut Mate uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect user information from unauthorized access, alteration, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Authentication secrets, OAuth refresh tokens, API keys, and other sensitive credentials should be stored on secure backend systems and must not be intentionally exposed in public client-side source code.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

15. Children's Privacy

CapCut Mate is not designed specifically for children under the age required for independent consent under applicable law.

CapCut Mate does not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling purposes.

16. Third-Party Services

CapCut Mate may interact with third-party services that have their own privacy policies and terms.

These may include:

  • Google Drive and Google APIs.
  • OpenAI or other AI processing providers.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers.

Users should review the applicable policies of these services when appropriate.

This Privacy Policy may be updated as CapCut Mate develops or as legal, technical, or platform requirements change.

When the policy changes, the effective date shown at the top of this page may be updated.
